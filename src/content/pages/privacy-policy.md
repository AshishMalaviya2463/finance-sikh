## Contact messages

When you use the contact form, we collect your name, email, optional subject and message to respond to your website enquiry. The form stores messages in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. Messages are not publicly readable and are not automatically emailed.

## Services and future advertising

Firebase is used only for submitted contact messages. Analytics is not installed by default. If Google AdSense is enabled, this policy must be updated to describe advertising cookies and third-party vendors, including [Google's advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
