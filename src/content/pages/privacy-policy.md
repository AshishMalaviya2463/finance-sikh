# Privacy Policy

**Updated: 9th November 2026**

Finance Sikh (we/us/our) is a personal finance education website. The website can be found at [https://financesikh.com/](https://financesikh.com/).

We value your privacy and want to be clear about what information we collect and how we use it whilst visiting our site, learning our educational content, using our calculators and contacting us.

This Privacy Policy details what information we collect, including our use of Google Analytics.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

When filling out our contact form, we collect the following information:

- Your name
- Email
- Subject (optional)
- Your message
- Date/time of submission

You are not required to fill out our contact form to read our articles or freely available calculators.

Do not include sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, payment card details, government ID numbers and any other personal information in your messages.

### Information Automatically Collected

When visiting our website, we may automatically collect some information from you using analytics tools and service providers.

This may include;

- Pages visited and interactions.
- Approximate geographical location based on technical information.
- Browser, device and operating system information.
- Referral and traffic information.
- Technical information and cookies.

We use this information to understand how our website is being used and improve our content.

## 2. Google Analytics

Finance Sikh uses a service called Google Analytics 4 (GA4).

This tool helps us understand how people find and interact with our website.

It may use cookies and other information to understand how our website is being used. Google may also process this information in line with their own Privacy Policy and service terms.

We do not intentionally send any personal information such as names, emails or messages from the contact form to Google Analytics.

For further information see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google - How we use information from sites and apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 3. Cookies & Similar Tools

We use cookies for analytics from Google Analytics.

These include cookies like;

- `_ga` – A cookie used to differentiate visitors to our website.
- `_ga_` – A cookie used by Google Analytics to store session information.

This helps us understand how people are interacting with our website.

You can control and delete cookies using the settings on your browser however, if you block certain cookies it could impact our analytics. You should still be able to access our educational content.

You can also look into the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

Other service providers may also use cookies, local storage or similar tools.

## 4. Contact Forms & Firebase

We use Google Firebase services to process and store our contact forms.

When you send us a message, information will be stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore so we can look into your enquiry and respond where needed.

Our integration with Firebase may also use security tools such as Firebase App Check and Google reCAPTCHA to help secure our contact form.

This information may be processed in line with Google’s privacy terms.

For further information see the [Firebase Privacy & Security documentation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 5. What We Do With Information

We use information collected via Finance Sikh to;

- Respond to your enquiry.
- Review any suggested changes or improvements.
- Understand website traffic and how people interact with our website.
- Improve our educational articles, calculators and website.
- Ensure our website remains secure and to resolve any technical issues.
- Comply with relevant legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent any personal information collected via our contact form.

We will not use information from our contact form to send marketing emails unless we have an appropriate and separate reason to do so.

## 6. Sharing Information With Third Parties

We may share or give access to personal information with third party service providers to allow us to run our website.

This may include Google Analytics, Google Firebase and website hosting, infrastructure and security providers.

We may also share information where it is reasonably necessary to comply with any applicable laws, respond to any lawful requests or to keep our website secure.

We will never publish any private information from our contact forms.

Personal information may be processed in line with the Privacy Policy of third-party services.

## 7. Storing & Securing Information

We take reasonable steps to keep the information we have secure.

However, we cannot guarantee that information sent over the internet or stored electronically is 100% secure.

We store contact form information for a reasonable period of time to help us deal with any enquiries, keep appropriate records and meet any legal obligations.

Analytics information will be stored in line with the settings and processing of Google Analytics.

Information may be processed and stored outside of India.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the relevant laws, you may have certain rights over your personal information. You may have the right to request access, correction and/or deletion.

To request any changes to your contact form information please email us at [financesikh.learn@gmail.com](mailto:financesikh.learn@gmail.com).

Please include enough information to let us know which submission you are referring to. We may need to confirm your request before taking any action.

You can control cookies using your browser settings. You can also use the available opt-outs for Google Analytics.

We will act on any relevant privacy requests in line with the relevant laws.

## 9. External Websites

Our articles may contain links to external websites including financial regulators, government departments, educational resources and information providers.

These websites are independent and may have their own privacy policies.

We recommend that you read their Privacy Policy before providing any personal information.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

We provide general educational information and are not specifically targeted towards children.

We do not intentionally try to collect any personal information from children using our contact form.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information please contact us so we can look into the request and take appropriate action.

## 11. Advertising

We may implement advertising services in the future.

If we do implement advertising services we will update this Privacy Policy to detail the advertising technologies, data and privacy options available.

This does not mean we currently have advertising services active.

## 12. Updating Our Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if there are any changes to our website features, service providers, data or relevant legal requirements.

We will publish an updated version of this Privacy Policy with an updated date.

We would recommend that you regularly check this page.
