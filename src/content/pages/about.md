Finance Sikh is an educational portal to help you understand
the basics of personal finance.

Personal finance can be complicated. Terminology, regulations and
features of various finance products can often confuse people. Finance
Sikh aims to simplify those topics by breaking them down into
straight forward topics with worked examples and helpful calculators.

This will help you learn more so that you understand what to think
about.

## Topics

We have lots of educational content on Finance Sikh. We cover:

- Budgeting: How to manage your money each month.
- Saving: How to save for emergencies. What to think about when saving
  and types of saving.
- Investing: Mutual Funds, SIPs, Compounding, Risk and Long Term
  Investment Principles.
- Insurance: Term Insurance, Health Insurance, Policy Features, Exclusions
  and Waiting Periods.
- Taxes: Tax Concepts in India, Tax Regimes, Deductions and Common Tax
  Terms.
- Loans: EMIs, Repaying your loans, Credit Scores and Cost of Borrowing.
- Calculators: Explore Financial Calculations using clearly defined
  assumptions.
  Note: The purpose of our content is to educate.

## What we aren’t

We provide general educational information. We are not offering any personal
investment, tax, insurance, legal, financial, or credit advice.
We do not give stock tips, we do not sell any financial products due to us
providing educational content. We are not a regulated financial advice
company. We do not recommend you use our articles and calculators instead of
looking at official documents.

## How we create our content

We aim to provide clear, practical and useful articles. We carry out
research using official publications, government websites, regulatory
documents, product documentation and other relevant primary resources
depending on the topic.
We aim to identify key assumptions in examples and calculators. We aim to
highlight general information and how this may differ to your own
circumstances. We may also update our content if there are changes to
regulations or information from the source.
It cannot be guaranteed that all details will always be up to date and
correct however we aim to check and update our content if we notice any
errors or have a reliable correction. If there are major updates we may
update the date of the article.
For more information please see our Editorial Policy.
