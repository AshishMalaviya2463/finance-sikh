# How To Improve Your CIBIL Score In India In 10 Steps

A poor CIBIL score can affect your ability to borrow. This can be anything from credit cards to personal loans and mortgages. However, your CIBIL score isn’t set in stone and you can improve it over time by being responsible with your borrowing.

There isn’t a quick way to Improve Your score. Instead, learn what’s on your report, what the problem areas are and work on improving your habits. This guide will give you some realistic ways to Improve Your CIBIL score in India. It won’t guarantee you’ll get approved for a loan and you shouldn’t take any personal credit advice.

## What Is A CIBIL Score?

A CIBIL score is a 3 digit score which summarises your credit history. This is based on your Credit Information Report (CIR). It will generally range between 300 and 900. Lenders can use this alongside your income, existing EMIs and their own lending criteria.

The report will show information about your credit accounts including any loans or credit cards. However, it won’t include any savings, fixed deposits or investments. A high score can mean you’re better with your credit but doesn’t guarantee you’ll get approved for a loan.

## What Is A Good CIBIL Score?

Scores of 700+ are generally considered a good score according to CIBIL. Lenders will have their own criteria for what they consider a good score and this can vary depending on the product and applicant. A score closer to 900 can help boost your profile.

However, if you have a score of below 700, then it’s a good idea to look at your report before applying again. You may have missed payments, high credit card balances, too many applications, a short credit history or errors.

## 10 Ways To Improve Your CIBIL Score

### 1. Pay All EMIs And Credit Card Bills On Time

Your payment history is an important part of your credit profile. If you don’t pay on time, lenders may see you as a higher risk. Try setting reminders a few days before your due dates. If you’re using auto debit, make sure you’ve got enough money in your account linked. You can also check that your payment went through.

Wherever possible try to pay the full amount on your credit card. Whilst paying the minimum can prevent you from having a missed payment, the balance can accrue interest.

### 2. Keep Credit Card Utilisation Low

Credit utilisation shows how much of your available limit you’re using. For example if you have a credit card with a limit of ₹1,00,000 and your balance is ₹70,000 then you’re using 70% of your available limit. High utilisation can suggest you’re reliant on your credit.

Try to keep your balances well below your limit. It’s commonly suggested to keep it under 30% however this isn’t a guaranteed CIBIL score. If you can, try to pay some of your balance before your statement date. Don’t overspend as your limit is increased.

### 3. Don’t Apply for Too Many Loans or Cards

When you apply for credit with a lender, they may perform a hard search. If you’re applying for multiple credits in a short space of time, you’ll have multiple searches.

Before applying compare the eligibility, costs and terms of your loan. Only use eligibility tools if you know whether this will be a soft or hard search. Only apply if you really need it.

### 4. Check Your CIBIL Report Regularly

Your report will show whether you have any overdue amounts, unknown accounts, incorrect information and searches. CIBIL will give you one free CIBIL Score and Report per year through the official site.

Check that your report shows any closed loans are correct, that the balances look correct and that payments are being recorded correctly. Keep any receipts, statements and loan closure letters. Start here to get your [free CIBIL score and report](https://www.cibil.com/freecibilscore).

### 5. Dispute Any Errors

Your report may show incorrect overdue amounts, loans you didn’t take out, duplicate accounts or payments which haven’t been updated. Dispute this with CIBIL and get in touch with the lender who provided the information.

CIBIL generally aren’t able to change the data provided by lenders. The credit institution will need to verify and approve any corrections. Provide any evidence and note down the reference number. Don’t pay any agents who promise to wipe correct negative information.

### 6. Pay Off Balances Strategically

Create a realistic repayment plan to pay off your balances. Pay the required amount by your due dates and if possible pay extra to balances which are expensive. If your account is overdue, get in touch with the lender through their official channels to confirm the amount, charges and options.

Don’t take on a new loan with high interest rates to Improve Your score. If you’re unable to pay the full amount, get in touch with the lender. Get any changes in writing and ensure you have proof of payment.

### 7. Be Responsible With Older Accounts

Having older accounts can help with your profile. If you have an older account with a consistent repayment history then this can show you have experience with credit.

Don’t close any older cards which you’ve been responsible with. Check the annual fee and terms first. If you keep it, make sure you’re using it within your budget and paying on time. Never keep a card which is expensive or not suitable just to Improve Your score.

### 8. Have A Healthy Credit Mix

Your report could have secured credit, such as a mortgage or vehicle loan. Or it could have unsecured credit, such as personal loans or credit cards. CIBIL advise to have a healthy mix but don’t take on too many unsecured loans.

Don’t take on credit just to create a mix. Only take on credit for a genuine reason and ensure you can afford to repay. If you don’t have a loan, don’t assume you need a personal loan to Improve Your score.

### 9. Be Responsible With Joint Accounts

If you’re missing payments on a joint, co-signed or guaranteed loan this can affect your credit history. Just because someone else is paying your monthly payment doesn’t mean they’re solely responsible.

Regularly check these accounts and get in touch before your due dates. If you’re a guarantor, make sure you understand what you’ve committed to.

### 10. Allow Time for Changes

Improving your credit habits and updating your report isn’t an instant process. Lenders will report information to the credit bureaux periodically. This means it could take some time for your payments or corrections to show. CIBIL state that it typically takes around 15-30 days for updates but can vary.

Continue to pay on time, keep your utilisation low and Check Your report after a reasonable amount of time. There isn’t a set time frame as it depends on your full credit history.

## How Long Does It Take To Improve My CIBIL Score?

There isn’t a set time frame. If you’ve recently had a payment reported it could show once the lender has updated the information. If you’ve had repeated late payments or a serious default it could take longer to improve. You’ll need to have a good repayment history.

Disputes can take around 30 days depending on the response from the credit institution. Keep track of your dispute. Just because you’ve requested it doesn’t mean it’ll show straight away. Beware of any services which promise to Improve Your score by a set amount in 30 days.

## Common Mistakes Which Can Affect Your CIBIL Score

- Missing payments on your EMIs.
- Using most of your available credit.
- Applying for multiple loans/cards in a short space of time.
- Ignoring entries on your report as you think your loan should be gone.
- Guaranteeing without keeping track of repayments.
- Paying an agent to Improve Your score by removing correct defaults.
- Taking on unnecessary loans to create a mix.

Instead try to Check Your report, keep a repayment schedule and only borrow when you understand the cost.

## How To Check Your CIBIL Score For Free

Go to the [official CIBIL site](https://www.cibil.com/freecibilscore). Choose the free CIBIL Score and Report option and register. You’ll need to verify your identity. CIBIL will give you one free score and report per year. Ensure you’re using the official site and don’t give any sensitive information such as your PAN, OTP, password etc. to unknown callers or links.

Once you’ve received your report Check Your personal details, account details, payment history, balances and search section. Save a copy of your report either by downloading or securely storing it. If you spot any discrepancies, dispute this through the official CIBIL process and get in touch with your lender.

## FAQs: How To Improve Your CIBIL Score

### 1. How Can I Improve My CIBIL Score Quickly?

Start by paying all of your current dues on time, pay down any high credit card balances and don’t apply unnecessarily. Check Your report for any errors and dispute any incorrect information. There’s no guaranteed quick fix. Improvements will depend on your credit history and how quickly lenders report updated information.

### 2. Can I Improve My CIBIL Score In 30 Days?

Some updates can show within 15-30 days once the lender has reported. However this doesn’t mean everyone’s score will improve within 30 days. If you have long standing late payments or defaults this may take longer to improve.

### 3. Does Checking My Own CIBIL Score Affect It?

Checking your own score through CIBIL is a self check. When you apply for a loan or credit card with a lender they will carry out a search. Regularly Check Your report and don’t apply unnecessarily.

### 4. What’s The Fastest Way To Improve My CIBIL Score From 600 To 750?

There isn’t a set way or time frame to Improve Your score from 600 to 750. Look at your report and identify why your score is low. Where possible pay down overdue amounts, keep your card utilisation low, dispute any errors and ensure you’re paying on time. This will depend on the details and how old the negative information is.

### 5. Does Paying The Minimum On My Credit Card Help?

Paying the minimum by your due date can help prevent you from having a missed payment. However it won’t remove the balance. You may still incur interest and high utilisation can affect your profile. It’s generally better to pay the full amount.

### 6. How Do I Correct Incorrect Loan/Payment Information?

Dispute through the official CIBIL site. Get in touch with the lender who reported the account. Provide any relevant statements, payment receipts or closure documents. CIBIL will work with the credit institution to verify. You can’t just ask for incorrect entries to be removed.

### 7. Can I Have A CIBIL Score If I’ve Never Taken Out A Loan Or Credit Card?

You may have no score or a ‘No Hit’. This is because you haven’t had enough credit activity. A credit score is based on your reported credit history. It won’t take into account your savings or salary. Don’t take on unnecessary debt to create a score.

## Final Thoughts

Improving your CIBIL score means creating a consistent history of responsible borrowing. Pay on time, keep your credit card balances low, be careful with your applications and Check Your report for any errors. Dispute any incorrect information.

Avoid any guaranteed improvements and don’t take on unnecessary debt. Allow time for updates and focus on improving your habits. Lenders will still look at your full financial situation.
